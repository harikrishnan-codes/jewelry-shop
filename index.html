Frequently Considered Inquiries

Everything you need to know regarding gemological certification, custom bespoke commissions, and private atelier viewings.

Every diamond above 0.50 carats is independently certified by the Gemological Institute of America (GIA) or Gubelin Laboratories. We strictly follow the Kimberley Process, sourcing 100% natural, untreated, and conflict-free gemstones paired with hallmark-authenticated 24K gold and 950 solid platinum.

A custom haute creation requires between 3 to 5 weeks. The journey begins with a private 3D render and gouache painting presentation, stone procurement, wax casting, and hand-pronging at our atelier. You receive weekly progress imagery and video dossiers directly from our master goldsmiths.

All shipments are fully insured at retail replacement value through Malca-Amit or Ferrari Logistics. Orders arrive in tamper-proof, armored packaging containing hand-crafted leather jewelry vaults, original micro-laser certification passports, and valuation appraisals for insurance underwriters.

Yes. We specialize in archival restorations and modern transformations. We preserve the sentimental integrity and provenance of antique stones while re-engineering structural settings into contemporary, comfortable, everyday silhouettes.

Private salon viewings are available by appointment at our atelier in Erode or through encrypted virtual consultations. You will be paired with a senior gemologist who prepares a curated tray of curated gemstones based on your aesthetic preferences.
